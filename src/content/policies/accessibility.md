## Our commitment

YMG Legal is committed to making our website and client portal usable by everyone, including people with disabilities. We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

## What we do

We use semantic page structure, text alternatives for meaningful images, sufficient color contrast, keyboard-accessible navigation and forms, visible focus indicators, and support for reduced-motion settings. We test with automated tools and manual keyboard and screen-reader checks.

## Known limitations

Some third-party tools, such as embedded booking calendars, may not fully meet these standards. We are working with providers to improve them.

## Feedback and assistance

If you have trouble accessing any part of our website, contact us at [info@ymg-legal.com](mailto:info@ymg-legal.com). Tell us the page and the problem, and we will provide the information in an accessible format and work to fix the issue. We aim to respond within [2] business days.
