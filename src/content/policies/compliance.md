## How we approach compliance

Legal marketing requires more than generating volume. YMG Legal structures its campaigns, intake workflows and support services with advertising, consent, privacy and professional-responsibility requirements in mind. This page explains the practices we build into our work. It is not legal advice, and each firm remains responsible for its own professional-responsibility obligations.

## We are not a law firm

YMG Legal is a legal marketing, case acquisition, intake and law firm support company. We do not provide legal advice, and contacting us does not create an attorney-client relationship. See our [Disclaimer](/disclaimer).

## Consent-aware acquisition

- Consumer forms collect express consent before any call or text, with the consent language shown next to the submit button.
- The consent text, time stamp and source page are recorded with each inquiry.
- Consent to be contacted is never required to purchase a product or service.

## TCPA-conscious practices

- Calls and texts are made only where the required consent has been given.
- Every text message includes opt-out instructions; STOP requests are honored promptly. See our [SMS Terms](/sms-terms).
- Do-not-call requests are recorded and respected across campaigns.

## Lead-source tracking

Each opportunity carries a record of where it came from, including the campaign, channel and landing page, so firms can see how and where a prospective client first engaged.

## Firm-defined criteria

Qualification is built around the criteria each firm sets, such as geography, timing, injury, treatment, liability and existing representation. Inquiries that do not meet those criteria are handled professionally and logged separately.

## Advertising and professional responsibility

Campaigns are prepared with state advertising rules in mind and reviewed with each participating firm before launch, including any disclaimers or responsible-attorney disclosures the firm requires.

## Secure data handling

Personal information is collected only for the purpose it was provided, shared only with the firms and service providers involved in that purpose, and protected with reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy).
