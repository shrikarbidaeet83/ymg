## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. We also use similar technologies such as pixels, tags and local storage (together, "cookies").

## 2. Cookies we use

| Category | Purpose | Examples [confirm actual tools] | Can you turn it off? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Strictly necessary | Site security, load balancing, form submission, portal login sessions | Session cookie, CSRF token | No |
| Analytics | Understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it | Google Analytics 4 | Yes |
| Advertising | Measure ad performance and show relevant ads | Google Ads, Meta Pixel | Yes |
| Functional | Remember preferences such as light/dark mode | Theme preference | Yes |
| Call tracking | Attribute calls to the campaign that generated them | [Call tracking provider] | Yes |

## 3. Your choices

Use the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer or the cookie banner to accept or reject non-essential cookies at any time. You can also block cookies in your browser settings; some features may not work. We honor Global Privacy Control signals as an opt-out of advertising cookies where required.

## 4. Third-party cookies

Some cookies are set by third parties such as Google and Meta, which process data under their own privacy policies.
