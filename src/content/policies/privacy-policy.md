## 1. Who we are

YMG Legal ("YMG Legal," "we," "us") is a legal marketing, case acquisition, intake and law firm support company based in Las Vegas, Nevada. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share and protect personal information through ymg-legal.com, our landing pages, phone lines, text messaging, client portal and related services (the "Services"). YMG Legal is not a law firm.

## 2. Information we collect

**Information you give us:** name, email, phone number, mailing address, law firm name and title (for business contacts), and information about a potential legal matter you choose to share, such as accident or injury details, dates, product or medication use, medical treatment, insurance, and existing representation.

**Information collected automatically:** IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed, referring URL, advertising and campaign identifiers (such as UTM parameters and click IDs), and information collected by cookies and similar technologies (see our [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy)).

**Call and message records:** if you call or text us, we may record calls and keep transcripts, call logs and message content for quality, training, compliance and dispute purposes. Where required by law, we will tell you at the start of a call that it is being recorded.

**Information from others:** advertising and analytics partners, data verification and fraud-prevention services, and publicly available sources.

## 3. How we use information

- To respond to inquiries and provide the Services
- To screen and evaluate whether a potential legal matter may meet the criteria of participating law firms
- To connect you with one or more participating law firms or attorneys, with your consent
- To contact you by phone, email or text, including with automated technology where you have consented
- To operate client accounts and the client portal for our law firm clients
- To measure and improve our marketing, websites and intake processes
- To prevent fraud, verify information and maintain security
- To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms

## 4. How we share information

**With participating law firms and attorneys.** When you submit information about a potential legal matter, you authorize us to share it with one or more law firms or attorneys who may contact you about your matter. Those firms use your information under their own privacy policies. Sharing your information with a law firm does not create an attorney-client relationship until a firm agrees to represent you.

**With service providers** who help us operate the Services (hosting, call centers, CRM, analytics, messaging, payment processing), bound by contractual confidentiality and security obligations.

**For legal reasons:** to comply with law, subpoenas or court orders, or to protect the rights, safety and property of YMG Legal, our clients or others.

**In a business transfer:** in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

## 8. Your privacy rights and choices {#your-choices}

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information; to obtain a copy; to opt out of targeted advertising, "sale" or "sharing" of personal information; to limit use of sensitive information; and to appeal a denied request. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

To make a request, email [privacy@ymg-legal.com](mailto:privacy@ymg-legal.com) [confirm address]. We will verify your request before acting on it. An authorized agent may submit a request on your behalf with proof of authorization. We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) browser signals as an opt-out where required.

**Marketing opt-out:** reply STOP to any text, use the unsubscribe link in any email, or tell our representative to stop calling.

## 9. Children

The Services are not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Information about a minor's potential legal matter should be submitted by a parent or legal guardian.

## 10. Third-party links

Our Services may link to third-party sites, including law firm websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.
